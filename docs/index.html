How it works

Nothing to learn.

Open it

Click the Hoops icon in the menu bar or press the shortcut.

Pull and release

Drag back from the ball. The longer the pull, the harder the shot.

Get back to work

Press Esc or click away and the court fades into the background.

Take a break. Take a shot.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Hoops is free and comes with three themes. Extra themes are $2.99 each, or $6.99 for all of them. It is a one-time payment.

How do I unlock a theme?

Click a locked theme in Appearance and choose Buy. You get a license key by email. Paste it into the app under "I already have a license".

Will it get in the way of my work?

No. Clicks pass through the court to the app below, and one key hides everything.

Which systems does it run on?

macOS (Apple Silicon) and Windows 10 or 11.

I lost my license key.

Email hoopsgame.app@gmail.com from the address you bought with and we will resend it.

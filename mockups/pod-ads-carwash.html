Car wash ads

Same 22x17 pixel screen as the other pod ads. Big view and actual size side by side.

A Drive-through

A muddy car rolls through a foam curtain with spinning brushes. The part that has passed is blue and clean, then a star twinkles on the roof.

B Spray and rinse

The car stands still while jets spray from both sides. It disappears under foam, the foam drops away, a glint sweeps over the paint and a star twinkles.

C Scrubbing sponge

A happy sponge scrubs back and forth over the car, leaving clean paint and soap bubbles behind. At the end it winks next to the star.

A painter on a rope paints the wall

He comes down from above the screen in a harness. This is the tall wall you get with "Fill the screen".

A Abseiler

He abseils down on one rope, swings along the wall and sprays a huge GO HOOPS across the bricks, one colour per letter. Then the rope pulls him back up.

B Hanging chair

He sits on a plank hanging from two ropes, with a paint bucket beside him, and rolls a big orange basketball onto the wall as the chair is lowered. Seams and shine come last.

C Swinging rainbow

One long rope from the roof. He swings in wide arcs across the wall like a pendulum and leaves a rainbow of paint along every swing, with drips. Then he is hauled up.

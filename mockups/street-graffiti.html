Street style, graffiti and a guy with a spray can

Same scene as in the game: brick wall, fence and fans. Drawn at the game's own pixel size.

A Tagging a name

The guy walks along the fence and sprays a bubble-letter HOOPS, one colour per letter, with white outline, shadow and drips. When it is done he shakes the can and the tag fades for the next round. A few old pieces sit on the wall above.

B A wall full of art

The whole wall is already covered: tags, a crown, a smiley, a stencil ball and fence tags. The guy strolls between spots, rattles the can and sprays a new star or lightning bolt on top, then walks on.

C Mural from a rolling scaffold

One big winged basketball painted in stages: outline, orange, seams, wings, shine and a signature. The guy stands on a stack of crates that rolls along as he works.

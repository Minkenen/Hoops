LED board, things to put on the dark wall

The wall behind the court is empty. Three ideas, each on the same wall with flags, crowd and ticker.

See how each one reacts.

A Jumbotron

A hanging scoreboard on cables with your score and shots, a streak page with flames and an equalizer page. A live ball cam on the left and a crowd wave on the right.

B Hall of fame

Retired jerseys and a championship banner hang from the roof and sway under spotlights. The numbers can be your milestones (25, 50, 100).

C Dot-matrix show

A wide LED wall that cycles equalizer bars, colour waves and a bouncing ball with a HOOPS sign. A goal fires fireworks over the whole wall.
